🌍 Earth and Environment

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain what the environment is.
  • Identify living and non-living parts of the environment.
  • Describe different types of environments.
  • Explain why plants, animals, water, air, and soil are important.
  • Identify causes of environmental pollution.
  • Explain ways of protecting the environment.

🌍 What Is the Environment?

The environment is everything around us that affects living things. It includes living things such as people, animals, and plants, as well as non-living things such as air, water, soil, rocks, and sunlight.

Human beings, animals, and plants depend on the environment for food, water, air, shelter, and other resources.

Remember: The environment includes both living and non-living things around us.

🌱 Living and Non-Living Parts of the Environment

🌳

Living Things

Living things are organisms that carry out life processes.

  • People
  • Animals
  • Plants
  • Birds
  • Insects
  • Microorganisms
☀️

Non-Living Things

Non-living things do not carry out biological life processes.

  • Air
  • Water
  • Soil
  • Rocks
  • Sunlight
  • Temperature

🌎 Important Parts of the Environment

💨 Air

Air surrounds the Earth. Humans and animals need oxygen in the air for respiration. Plants also use gases from the air to make their food.

💧 Water

Water is essential for all living things. People need water for drinking, cooking, washing, and many other activities.

🌱 Soil

Soil supports plant growth. It contains minerals, water, air, and organic matter.

☀️ Sunlight

Sunlight provides energy for life on Earth. Plants use sunlight to make food through photosynthesis.

🌳 Plants

Plants provide food and oxygen and offer shelter to many animals. They also help protect the soil.

🐘 Animals

Animals are important parts of ecosystems. They interact with plants, other animals, and their environment.

🏞️ Habitats

A habitat is the natural place where an organism lives. It provides the food, water, shelter, and other conditions that the organism needs to survive.

🌳

Forest

Forests contain many trees, plants, insects, birds, and other animals.

🏜️

Desert

Deserts receive very little rainfall and contain organisms adapted to dry conditions.

🌊

Water Habitat

Rivers, lakes, oceans, and wetlands provide habitats for many aquatic organisms.

🌾

Grassland

Grasslands contain large areas of grasses and support many herbivores and predators.

🔗 Simple Food Chains

A food chain shows how energy and food pass from one organism to another.

Example:

🌱 Grass → 🦗 Grasshopper → 🐸 Frog → 🐍 Snake

In this example, the grasshopper eats the grass, the frog eats the grasshopper, and the snake eats the frog.

Important: Plants are usually producers because they make their own food. Animals obtain energy by eating plants or other animals.

🏭 Environmental Pollution

Pollution occurs when harmful substances are introduced into the environment and cause damage to living things or natural resources.

💨

Air Pollution

Air pollution occurs when harmful gases, smoke, or particles enter the air.

Example: Smoke from vehicles and factories.

💧

Water Pollution

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances contaminate rivers, lakes, oceans, or other water sources.

Example: Dumping waste into a river.

🗑️

Land Pollution

Land pollution can happen when rubbish and harmful materials are left on the ground.

Example: Throwing plastic waste everywhere.

Why is pollution dangerous?

Pollution can harm humans, animals, plants, soil, water, and air. It can also damage habitats and ecosystems.

♻️ Protecting the Environment

Everyone has a responsibility to care for the environment.

♻️

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

We can reduce waste by using resources carefully, reusing useful items, and recycling materials.

🌳

Plant Trees

Trees provide habitats, help protect soil, and absorb carbon dioxide.

🚮

Dispose of Waste Properly

Waste should be placed in appropriate bins and should not be thrown into rivers or streets.

💧

Save Water

We should avoid wasting clean water and protect water sources from pollution.

☁️ Weather and the Environment

Weather describes the conditions of the atmosphere at a particular time and place.

Weather can include temperature, rainfall, wind, cloud cover, and sunshine.

Example:

If it is raining heavily today, we can say that the weather is rainy.

Changes in weather can affect people, plants, animals, farming, transport, and many other activities.
