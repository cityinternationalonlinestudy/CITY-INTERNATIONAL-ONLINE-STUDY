🌱 Environment and Resources

Social Studies Lesson 5: Learn about the environment, natural resources, their importance, environmental problems and ways of protecting our environment.

🎯 Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain the meaning of environment.
  • Identify natural and human-made resources.
  • Explain why resources are important.
  • Identify some environmental problems.
  • Explain ways of conserving natural resources.
  • Describe how people can protect their communities.

🌍 What Is the Environment?

The environment is everything around us that affects living things. It includes natural things such as air, water, soil, plants, animals, rivers, lakes and forests.

The environment also includes things made or changed by people, such as buildings, roads, schools and bridges.

🌳 Living Things

Plants, animals and people are living things found in the environment.

💧 Water

Water is important for drinking, farming, cleaning and other activities.

🌬️ Air

Living things need clean air to survive.

🌱 Soil

Soil supports plants and is important for farming.

🌿 Natural Resources

Natural resources are useful things that come from nature. People depend on them for food, shelter, energy and other needs.

💧

Water

Used for drinking, farming, cooking, cleaning and many other activities.

🌳

Forests

Provide habitats for animals and useful materials while helping the environment.

🌱

Soil

Supports plant growth and agricultural activities.

🐟

Wildlife

Animals are important to ecosystems and tourism.

☀️

Sunlight

Provides energy that supports life and can also be used to produce solar power.

⛏️

Minerals

Minerals found in the ground can be useful for different industries and activities.

⭐ Importance of Natural Resources

Natural resources are important because people and other living things depend on them.

  • They provide food.
  • They provide water.
  • They provide materials for building.
  • They support farming.
  • They provide habitats for wildlife.
  • They support tourism.
  • They can provide sources of energy.
  • They support people's livelihoods.

⚠️ Environmental Problems

The environment can be damaged when people use resources carelessly or pollute the surroundings.

🗑️ Poor Waste Disposal

Throwing rubbish in inappropriate places can pollute land and water.

🌳 Deforestation

Cutting down too many trees can damage forests and wildlife habitats.

💧 Water Pollution

Waste and harmful substances can make water unsafe.

🌫️ Air Pollution

Smoke and other harmful substances can reduce air quality.

♻️ Conservation of the Environment

Conservation means protecting and using natural resources carefully so that they remain available for the future.

Ways to Protect the Environment

  • Plant and protect trees.
  • Keep water sources clean.
  • Dispose of waste properly.
  • Reduce unnecessary pollution.
  • Protect wildlife and their habitats.
  • Use water carefully.
  • Reuse useful materials when possible.
  • Keep schools and communities clean.

🌱 Our Responsibility

Children and adults can all help protect the environment. Small actions such as planting trees, keeping surroundings clean and avoiding pollution can make a difference.
