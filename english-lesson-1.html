English Lesson 1

Parts of Speech

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain what parts of speech are.
  • Identify common parts of speech.
  • Recognize nouns and pronouns.
  • Identify verbs and adjectives.
  • Identify adverbs, prepositions and conjunctions.
  • Use different parts of speech correctly in sentences.

1. What Are Parts of Speech?

Definition: Parts of speech are groups of words classified according to the work they do in a sentence.

Every word in a sentence has a job. Some words name people, places or things. Some show actions. Others describe things or connect words together.

Learning parts of speech helps us understand and write correct English sentences.

2. Noun

Definition: A noun is a word that names a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Examples

  • Teacher
  • Uganda
  • School
  • Book
  • Dog
  • Happiness
Sentence: The teacher opened the book.

3. Pronoun

Definition: A pronoun is a word used instead of a noun.

Common Pronouns

  • I
  • You
  • He
  • She
  • It
  • We
  • They
Example: Sarah is a student. She studies English.

The word she replaces the noun Sarah.

4. Verb

Definition: A verb is a word that shows an action or a state of being.

Action Verbs

  • Run
  • Jump
  • Write
  • Read
  • Eat
  • Sing

Examples

The children play football.
Mary reads a book.

5. Adjective

Definition: An adjective is a word that describes a noun or pronoun.

Examples

  • Big
  • Small
  • Beautiful
  • Red
  • Fast
  • Happy
The girl has a beautiful dress.
He drives a fast car.

6. Adverb

Definition: An adverb is a word that describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.

Examples

  • Quickly
  • Slowly
  • Carefully
  • Loudly
  • Yesterday
  • Very
The boy ran quickly.
She speaks slowly.

7. Preposition

Definition: A preposition is a word that shows the relationship between words, often showing place, time or direction.

Examples

  • In
  • On
  • Under
  • Behind
  • Near
  • Between
The book is on the table.
The cat is under the chair.

8. Conjunction

Definition: A conjunction is a word that joins words, phrases or sentences.

Common Conjunctions

  • And
  • But
  • Or
  • Because
  • So
John and Mary are friends.
I wanted to play, but it was raining.

9. Interjection

Definition: An interjection is a word or short expression that expresses a strong feeling or emotion.

Examples

  • Wow!
  • Oh!
  • Hurrah!
  • Ouch!
  • Hurray!
Wow! That is a beautiful picture.

10. Parts of Speech Summary

Noun

Names a person, place, thing or idea.

Pronoun

Replaces a noun.

Verb

Shows an action or state of being.

Adjective

Describes a noun or pronoun.

Adverb

Describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.

Preposition

Shows a relationship between words.

Conjunction

Joins words, phrases or sentences.

Interjection

Expresses strong feeling or emotion.
