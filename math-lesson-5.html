  • Understand what a mathematical word problem is.

🧠 What is Problem Solving?

Problem solving means using mathematical knowledge and logical thinking to find an answer to a problem.

Many mathematical problems are written as stories. These are called word problems.

To solve a word problem, we need to understand what the question is asking before choosing an operation.

➕➖✖️➗ The Four Basic Operations

+

Addition

Used when quantities are combined or increased.

−

Subtraction

Used when finding the difference or taking away.

×

Multiplication

Used when equal groups are combined.

÷

Division

Used when sharing or grouping equally.

🪜 Steps for Solving a Problem

1 Read the Problem

Read the problem carefully. Make sure you understand what is happening.

2 Find the Important Information

Look for the numbers and information that are needed to solve the problem.

3 Identify the Question

Ask yourself: What do I need to find?

4 Choose an Operation

Decide whether you need addition, subtraction, multiplication or division.

5 Calculate

Do the mathematical calculation carefully.

6 Check Your Answer

Think about whether your answer makes sense.

📘 Worked Example 1 — Addition

Sarah has 25 books. Her teacher gives her 18 more books.

How many books does Sarah have altogether?


We are combining two quantities, so we use addition.

25 + 18 = 43

Answer: Sarah has 43 books.

📘 Worked Example 2 — Subtraction

A school has 80 computers. If 25 computers are being used in the laboratory, how many computers are not being used?


We are finding what remains, so we use subtraction.

80 − 25 = 55

Answer: 55 computers are not being used.

📘 Worked Example 3 — Multiplication

A teacher gives 6 students 4 pencils each. How many pencils are needed?


There are equal groups, so we use multiplication.

6 × 4 = 24

Answer: 24 pencils are needed.

📘 Worked Example 4 — Division

A teacher has 30 books and wants to share them equally among 5 students.


We are sharing equally, so we use division.

30 ÷ 5 = 6

Answer: Each student receives 6 books.

🧩 Multi-Step Problems

Some problems require more than one operation. These are called multi-step problems.

A school buys 5 boxes of pencils. Each box contains 12 pencils. The school gives 15 pencils to students. How many pencils remain?


Step 1: Find the total number of pencils.

5 × 12 = 60


Step 2: Subtract the pencils given to students.

60 − 15 = 45


Answer: 45 pencils remain.
