Writing Skills

Learn how to organize ideas, write clear sentences, build paragraphs, and communicate effectively through writing.

🎯 Lesson Objectives

  • Understand the importance of good writing.
  • Write clear and complete sentences.
  • Organize ideas into paragraphs.
  • Use correct capitalization and punctuation.
  • Use linking words to connect ideas.
  • Write a short descriptive paragraph.
  • Edit and improve your own writing.

📖 What Are Writing Skills?

Writing skills are the skills we use to communicate our thoughts, ideas, information, and feelings through written words.

Good writing should be:
  • Clear
  • Organized
  • Correct
  • Interesting
  • Easy to understand

✏️ 1. Write Complete Sentences

A good piece of writing begins with complete sentences. Each sentence should express a complete thought.

❌ Because I was tired.

This is not a complete sentence.
✅ I went to bed early because I was tired.

📝 2. Writing a Paragraph

A paragraph is a group of related sentences about one main idea.

Parts of a Paragraph

Topic Sentence

Introduces the main idea.

Supporting Sentences

Give details, examples, and explanations.

Closing Sentence

Brings the paragraph to a suitable ending.

🌱 Example Paragraph

My School Garden


Our school garden is a beautiful place where students can learn about plants. We grow vegetables such as tomatoes, onions, and carrots. Students help to water the plants and remove weeds. The garden also teaches us to care for our environment. I enjoy spending time in our school garden because it is both useful and fun.

Notice that every sentence in the paragraph is connected to the main topic: the school garden.

🔗 3. Linking Words

Linking words help connect ideas and make writing easier to follow.

Adding ideas:

and, also, furthermore, in addition
Showing contrast:

but, however, although
Showing reason:

because, since
Showing result:

therefore, so, as a result

🔠 4. Capitalization and Punctuation

Correct capitalization and punctuation make writing clear and easy to read.

❌ my name is john. i live in kampala

✅ My name is John. I live in Kampala.

Remember to capitalize names of people, places, countries, cities, and the beginning of sentences.

🎨 5. Descriptive Writing

Descriptive writing uses words that help the reader imagine a person, place, object, or event.

Example:

The garden was bright and beautiful. Tall green plants stood beside colorful flowers. The fresh smell of flowers filled the warm morning air.

Descriptive words make writing more interesting.

🔍 6. Editing Your Writing

Always read your writing again after you finish. Look for mistakes and improve your ideas.

Editing Checklist:
  • Did I use complete sentences?
  • Did I start sentences with capital letters?
  • Did I use the correct punctuation?
  • Are my ideas organized?
  • Did I spell words correctly?
  • Does my paragraph stay on one main topic?

🔄 The Writing Process

1. Plan

Think about your topic and ideas.

2. Draft

Write your first version.

3. Revise

Improve your ideas and organization.

4. Edit

Correct grammar, spelling, capitalization, and punctuation.

5. Publish

Prepare your final version.

📌 Lesson Summary

  • Good writing communicates ideas clearly.
  • Use complete sentences.
  • A paragraph should focus on one main idea.
  • Use supporting details to explain your ideas.
  • Use linking words to connect ideas.
  • Use correct capitalization and punctuation.
  • Edit your writing before submitting it.
  • Follow the writing process: plan, draft, revise, edit, publish.
