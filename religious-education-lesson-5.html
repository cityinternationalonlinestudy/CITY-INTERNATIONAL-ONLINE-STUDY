Lesson 5

🤝 Responsibility and Service

Learn how responsibility, helpfulness and service can make our families, schools and communities stronger.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain what responsibility means.
  • Identify responsibilities at home and at school.
  • Explain the meaning of service.
  • Give examples of helping other people.
  • Explain why responsible behaviour is important.

📚 What Is Responsibility?

Responsibility means being willing to do what you are expected to do and accepting the results of your actions.

Responsible people try to complete their duties, keep promises and take care of things that have been placed in their care.

Example:

A responsible student completes schoolwork, follows school rules, respects others and takes care of school property.

🏠 Responsibility at Home and School

🏠
At Home

Helping with suitable household tasks, keeping your room tidy and caring for belongings.

🏫
At School

Completing assignments, attending lessons, respecting teachers and caring for school property.

🌳
In the Community

Keeping the environment clean and treating neighbours with respect.

👥
With Others

Keeping promises, being honest and helping people when appropriate.

❤️ What Is Service?

Service means helping other people or contributing to the community without expecting something in return.

Examples of service include:

  • Helping an elderly person with a safe task.
  • Helping a classmate understand schoolwork.
  • Keeping shared places clean.
  • Sharing appropriate resources with someone in need.
  • Taking part in useful community activities.
  • Helping family members with suitable tasks.

🌟 Good Service Requires Good Character

Service is most meaningful when it is done with care, respect and good intentions.

💙
Kindness

Treat people with care and consideration.

🤝
Cooperation

Work together to achieve useful goals.

⏰
Commitment

Do your responsibilities consistently.

🙏
Respect

Value other people's dignity and rights.

🧭 Making Responsible Choices

Before making an important choice, think about how your actions may affect yourself and other people.

1. Think

Ask yourself what you are about to do.

2. Consider the consequences

Think about what might happen because of your choice.

3. Choose what is right

Choose an action that is safe, respectful and responsible.

4. Take responsibility

If you make a mistake, admit it and try to correct it.
