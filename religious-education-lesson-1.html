Lesson 1

🙏 God and Creation

Learn about God, creation, the world around us, gratitude and our responsibility to care for creation.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain what creation means.
  • Identify things found in creation.
  • Explain why people should care for the environment.
  • Show appreciation and gratitude for life and creation.
  • Give examples of responsible actions toward creation.

📖 What Is Creation?

Creation refers to the world and everything that exists in it. Religious traditions teach that God is the source of creation and that human beings have a responsibility to care for the world around them.

We can observe creation in many forms, including people, animals, plants, water, land, the sky and other parts of nature.

Remember:

Creation should be treated with care, respect and responsibility.

🌍 Things We See in Creation

There are many things around us that help us appreciate the world in which we live.

🌳 Plants

Trees, flowers, grass and crops.

🐘 Animals

Birds, insects, fish and other animals.

💧 Water

Rivers, lakes, rain and other sources of water.

🌞 Natural Features

Mountains, soil, sunlight and the sky.

👨‍👩‍👧 People and Creation

Human beings depend on the natural world for many things. We need clean air, water, food and safe places to live.

Because people depend on creation, we should use natural resources wisely and avoid actions that damage the environment.

Examples of responsible actions

  • Planting and protecting trees.
  • Keeping our homes and schools clean.
  • Avoiding unnecessary waste.
  • Protecting animals and their habitats.
  • Using water carefully.
  • Keeping rivers and other water sources clean.

❤️ Gratitude and Respect

Many religious teachings encourage people to be thankful for life and for the things they receive.

Gratitude can be shown through words, actions and responsible behaviour.

Example:

A student can show respect for creation by keeping the school compound clean and protecting plants.
