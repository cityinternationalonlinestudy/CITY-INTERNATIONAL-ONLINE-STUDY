Reading Comprehension

Learn how to read carefully, understand information, find important details, and answer questions from a passage.

🎯 Lesson Objectives

  • Understand what reading comprehension means.
  • Identify the main idea of a passage.
  • Find important details in a text.
  • Understand the meaning of words from context.
  • Answer questions using information from a passage.
  • Make simple conclusions from what you read.

📖 What Is Reading Comprehension?

Reading comprehension is the ability to read a text, understand its meaning, remember important information, and answer questions about what you have read.

Remember: Good readers do not only read the words. They think about what the words mean.

🧠 Reading Strategies

1. Read Carefully

Read the passage slowly enough to understand what is happening.

2. Find the Main Idea

Ask yourself: "What is this passage mainly about?"

3. Look for Details

Find facts, names, places, numbers, and events that support the main idea.

4. Look at the Context

If you do not know a word, use the words around it to help you understand its meaning.

5. Read the Question Carefully

Make sure you understand what the question is asking before choosing an answer.

📚 Reading Passage

A Visit to the School Garden


One Saturday morning, a group of students from City International Online School visited a community garden near their school. Their teacher, Mr. Daniel, organized the visit so that the students could learn more about plants and the environment.

When they arrived, the students saw many different plants growing in neat rows. Some plants were vegetables, while others produced fruits. The students were surprised to see how much food could be grown in a small area.

Mr. Daniel explained that plants need sunlight, water, air, and suitable soil to grow well. He also explained that taking care of plants helps protect the environment.

The students then helped the gardeners remove weeds and water the plants. They worked together and enjoyed the activity. Before leaving, the students promised to start a small garden at their school.

🔎 Understanding the Passage

Main Idea:

The students visited a community garden to learn about plants and caring for the environment.

Important Details:
  • The visit happened on a Saturday.
  • Mr. Daniel organized the visit.
  • The students saw vegetables and fruit plants.
  • Plants need sunlight, water, air, and suitable soil.
  • The students helped remove weeds and water plants.

✏️ Practice Questions

1. Why did Mr. Daniel organize the visit?

Answer: To help students learn about plants and the environment.
2. Name two things plants need to grow.

Answer: Sunlight and water.
3. What did the students do in the garden?

Answer: They removed weeds and watered the plants.
4. What did the students promise to do?

Answer: They promised to start a small garden at their school.
