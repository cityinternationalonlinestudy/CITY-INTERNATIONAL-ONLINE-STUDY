⚡ Energy and Forces

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain what energy is.
  • Identify common forms of energy.
  • Explain how energy is used in everyday life.
  • Explain what a force is.
  • Identify pushes and pulls as forces.
  • Describe how forces can change the movement or shape of objects.

⚡ What Is Energy?

Energy is the ability to cause change or do work. Everything we do requires energy.

We need energy to walk, run, think, play, cook food, operate machines, and use electronic devices.

Remember: Energy can be transferred from one object or system to another and can change from one form to another.

🔋 Forms of Energy

☀️

Light Energy

Light energy allows us to see. The Sun is a major natural source of light.

Examples: Sunlight, lamps, candles.

🔊

Sound Energy

Sound energy is produced when objects vibrate.

Examples: Speaking, musical instruments, bells.

🔥

Heat Energy

Heat energy makes objects warmer.

Examples: Fire, hot water, sunlight.

🔋

Electrical Energy

Electrical energy powers many devices and machines.

Examples: Computers, televisions, refrigerators.

🏃

Movement Energy

Moving objects have energy associated with their motion.

Examples: A moving bicycle, running person, moving car.

🍞

Chemical Energy

Chemical energy is stored in substances.

Examples: Food, fuels, and batteries.

🌞 Sources of Energy

Energy comes from many different sources. Some sources are renewable and some are non-renewable.

☀️

Solar Energy

Energy from the Sun can provide light and heat and can be converted into electricity.

💨

Wind Energy

Moving air can be used to turn turbines and generate electricity.

💧

Hydroelectric Energy

Moving water can be used to generate electricity.

🪨

Fossil Fuels

Coal, oil, and natural gas are examples of fossil fuels. They are non-renewable resources.

🔄 Energy Changes

Energy can change from one form into another.

Example 1: Electric Bulb

Electrical energy is changed mainly into light and heat energy when a bulb is switched on.

Example 2: Human Body

Chemical energy stored in food can be changed into movement and heat when we exercise.

Example 3: Solar Panel

Solar panels convert energy from sunlight into electrical energy.

💪 What Is a Force?

A force is a push or a pull that can change the motion or shape of an object.

Simple definition: Force = Push or Pull

Forces can make objects start moving, stop moving, speed up, slow down, change direction, or change shape.

👉 Pushes and Pulls

👉

Push

A push moves an object away from you.

Examples:

  • Pushing a door open.
  • Pushing a box.
  • Pushing a shopping cart.
👈

Pull

A pull moves an object toward you.

Examples:

  • Pulling a drawer.
  • Pulling a rope.
  • Pulling a door closed.

🎯 Effects of Forces

A force can have several effects on an object.

▶️

Start Movement

A force can make a stationary object begin to move.

Example: Kicking a stationary football.

🛑

Stop Movement

A force can stop a moving object.

Example: Catching a moving ball.

↪️

Change Direction

A force can change the direction of movement.

Example: Hitting a moving ball with a bat.

🧲

Change Shape

A force can change the shape of an object.

Example: Squeezing a sponge.

🌍 Gravity

Gravity is a force that pulls objects toward the Earth.

Gravity is why objects fall toward the ground when they are dropped.

Example:

If you drop a ball from your hand, gravity pulls the ball toward the ground.

🛞 Friction

Friction is a force that opposes the motion of objects when two surfaces rub against each other.

Example:

When a bicycle moves along a road, friction between the tyres and the road helps the bicycle maintain grip.

Important: Friction can be useful, but it can also slow moving objects and cause surfaces to wear.

⚖️ Balanced and Unbalanced Forces

Balanced Forces

Balanced forces are forces that cancel each other out. They do not cause a change in an object's motion.

Unbalanced Forces

Unbalanced forces can cause an object's motion to change.

Example:

If two people push a box with equal force in opposite directions, the forces may be balanced and the box may remain still.
