Living and Non-Living Things

Learn how to identify living and non-living things and understand the characteristics that make something living.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Define living and non-living things.
  • Identify examples of living things.
  • Identify examples of non-living things.
  • Understand the characteristics of living things.
  • Compare living and non-living things.
  • Classify objects as living or non-living.

🌱 What Are Living Things?

Living things are things that have life. They carry out life processes such as growing, breathing, feeding and reproducing.

Living things can be found everywhere around us. People, animals, plants and many tiny organisms are living things.

🌿 Examples of Living Things

👨

Human Beings

Humans breathe, grow, eat, move and reproduce.

🐕

Animals

Animals eat, breathe, grow and reproduce.

🌳

Plants

Plants grow, reproduce and make their own food.

🦠

Microorganisms

Many microorganisms are living organisms too.

🔬 Characteristics of Living Things

Living things have several important characteristics.

1. Movement

Living things can move or show movement. Animals can move from one place to another. Plants can also show movement, such as turning toward light.

2. Respiration

Living things release energy from food through respiration.

3. Sensitivity

Living things can detect and respond to changes in their surroundings.

4. Growth

Living things increase in size and develop as they grow.

5. Reproduction

Living things can produce new organisms of their own kind.

6. Excretion

Living things remove waste products from their bodies.

7. Nutrition

Living things need nutrients or food to obtain energy and support growth.

🪨 What Are Non-Living Things?

Non-living things are things that do not have life and do not carry out the life processes of living things.

Examples include rocks, chairs, tables, books, pencils, water and many machines.

🪨

Rock

A rock is non-living.

📚

Book

A book is non-living.

🪑

Chair

A chair is non-living.

✏️

Pencil

A pencil is non-living.

⚖️ Living vs Non-Living Things

Living Things Non-Living Things
Have life Do not have life
Grow Do not grow naturally
Need nutrients Do not need food
Respire Do not respire
Reproduce Do not reproduce
Respond to stimuli Do not respond biologically

💡 Important Idea

Some non-living things can appear to move, but movement alone does not mean something is living.


For example, a car can move, but a car is not a living thing because it does not grow, reproduce or carry out biological life processes.
