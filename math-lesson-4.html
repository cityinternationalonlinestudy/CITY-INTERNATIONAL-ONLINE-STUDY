📐 What is Geometry?

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that studies shapes, sizes, positions, lines, angles and spaces.

Geometry is used in many areas of everyday life. Architects, engineers, builders, designers and artists all use geometry.

🔷 Basic Geometric Shapes

○

□

▭

📏 Points and Lines

1. Point

A point represents an exact position or location.

Example: • A

2. Line

A line is straight and continues infinitely in both directions.

Example: ←────────────→

3. Line Segment

A line segment has two endpoints.

Example: A────────B

4. Ray

A ray has one endpoint and continues infinitely in one direction.

Example: A────────────→

📐 Types of Angles

Acute Angle

An acute angle is less than 90°.

Acute angle < 90°

Right Angle

A right angle is exactly 90°.

Right angle = 90°

Obtuse Angle

An obtuse angle is greater than 90° but less than 180°.

90° < Obtuse angle < 180°

Straight Angle

A straight angle is exactly 180°.

Straight angle = 180°

📏 Perimeter

The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a shape.

Rectangle Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Width)
Square Perimeter = 4 × Side
Example:

A rectangle has a length of 8 cm and a width of 3 cm.

Perimeter = 2 × (8 + 3)

Perimeter = 2 × 11

Answer = 22 cm

⬜ Area

The area is the amount of space inside a two-dimensional shape.

Area of a Rectangle

Area = Length × Width

Area of a Square

Area = Side × Side

Area of a Triangle

Area = ½ × Base × Height
Example:

A rectangle has a length of 10 cm and a width of 4 cm.

Area = 10 × 4

Answer = 40 cm²

Example:

A triangle has a base of 8 cm and a height of 5 cm.

Area = ½ × 8 × 5

Area = 20 cm²

Answer = 20 cm²
